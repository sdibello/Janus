# 0003: Keep initial campaigns and character lists minimal

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Status: Accepted
- Related specs: [002: Campaigns and character lists](../specs/002-campaigns-and-characters.md)
- Supersedes: None
- Superseded by: None

## Context

The project owner requested campaigns consisting of a name and creation date, with the ability to add PCs and NPCs to each campaign and maintain those lists.

## Decision

Keep initial campaign information limited to name and creation date, with associated lists of PCs and NPCs. Do not require a game system or full character sheets for this capability.

This records the requested scope. Character fields, access defaults, date assignment, and the exact maintenance actions are proposals or open questions in the draft spec, not settled decisions.

## Alternatives considered

- Requiring detailed campaign configuration or full character sheets at creation: beyond the requested initial scope.

## Consequences

Users can begin organizing campaigns with minimal information. Richer character information and campaign collaboration can be specified separately without making them prerequisites for campaign creation.
