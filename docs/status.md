# 🧭 D&D 3.5 Conditions — Status + Mechanical Impact (Markdown)

## **Ability / Attribute Conditions**
- **Ability Damaged** — Temporary ability score loss; penalties apply immediately.
- **Ability Drained** — Permanent ability score loss; cannot be healed normally.
- **Energy Drained** — Gain negative levels; –1 to attacks, saves, skills; may lose a character level.

## **Perception & Awareness**
- **Blinded** — 50% miss chance; –2 AC; loses Dex bonus to AC; –4 Search/Spot; moves at half speed.
- **Dazzled** — –1 attack rolls, Search, and Spot.
- **Deafened** — –4 initiative; 20% spell failure for verbal spells; –10 Listen.

## **Movement & Position**
- **Blown Away** — Knocked prone and pushed back by strong wind.
- **Checked** — Movement stopped by strong wind; cannot move forward.
- **Entangled** — –2 attack; –4 Dex; movement halved; cannot run or charge.
- **Flat-Footed** — Cannot use Dex bonus to AC; cannot make attacks of opportunity.
- **Grappling** — –4 attack vs non-grappled; limited actions; no Dex bonus to AC.
- **Pinned** — Immobilized; only limited actions; –4 AC.
- **Prone** — –4 melee attack; +4 AC vs ranged; must stand to move normally.
- **Stunned** — Cannot act; drops held items; –2 AC.
- **Paralyzed** — Helpless; cannot move; can be coup de graced.
- **Helpless** — Treated as Dex 0; opponents get +4 to hit; subject to coup de grace.

## **Mental / Control Effects**
- **Confused** — Random actions each round (attack nearest, babble, flee, etc.).
- **Cowering** — Frozen; –2 AC; loses Dex bonus to AC.
- **Dazed** — Cannot act for 1 round; no AC penalty.
- **Fascinated** — Cannot act except to watch the source; –4 to skill checks vs distractions.
- **Frightened** — Must flee; –2 attack, saves, skill checks.
- **Panicked** — Drops items; flees; cowers if cornered; –2 attack, saves, skill checks.
- **Shaken** — –2 attack, saves, skill checks.
- **Staggered** — Only one move or standard action per round.

## **Life & Health States**
- **Dead** — No actions; body becomes a corpse.
- **Disabled** — At 0 HP; one move or standard action per round; further action risks falling unconscious.
- **Dying** — –1 to –9 HP; unconscious; loses 1 HP per round.
- **Unconscious** — Helpless; no actions; prone.

---

## **Fatigue & Exhaustion**
- **Fatigued** — Cannot run or charge; –2 Str/Dex.
- **Exhausted** — Move at half speed; –6 Str/Dex; becomes fatigued after rest.

---

## **Environmental / Special**
- **Dehydrated** — Typically fatigued; may take nonlethal damage.
- **Nauseated** — Can only take a single move action per round.
- **Sickened** — –2 attack, damage, saves, skill checks, ability checks.
- **Poisoned** — Ability damage or other effects depending on poison.
- **Diseased** — Ability damage or special effects depending on disease.

---

## **Combat & Spellcasting**
- **Turned** — Undead flee for 10 rounds.
- **Rebuked** — Undead cower for 10 rounds.
- **Stunned** — (Listed above) Cannot act; drops items; –2 AC.

---

## **Other SRD Conditions**
- **Petrified** — Turned to stone; unconscious; hardness 8; immune to most effects.
- **Incorporeal** — Immune to nonmagical attacks; 50% chance to ignore magical attacks.
- **Invisible** — Total concealment (50% miss chance); +2 attack; denies Dex bonus to foes.
