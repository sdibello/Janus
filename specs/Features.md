# Possible New Features
## Active Battle Screen
### Spell Effects
  * During an active battle tracking the impact of spells during the battle.  A new button between "Add Participant" and "End Encounter" called "Spell Effect"
  * In a little light box a spell can be added to multiple PCs/NPCs/Mobs.  The user can add a name, effect, and duration as well.
  * The spell name will be added in a bubble in the area where statues are shown.
  * The spell impact will be show where the other status impacts are displayed for the active participant.
  * There will be a new box in the right column between Hold and Statuses where the active spells will be displayed.  Just the name of the spell shown similar to the statues.  When you mouse over that name, it will show the spell effect.
  * When creating a spell effect, you must choose the caster.  The spells duration will be tracked off the turns of the caster, and will be removed when the duration is spent.
  * The Caster can be any participant in the battle.

### Summoned Creatures.
* During an active battle, tracking summoned creatures during the battle.  These creatures appear during the battle, attack on the round they where summoned, and dissappear after their duraction has been spent.
* A "Summon Creature" appears immediatly after the participant that summoned it.
* A summoned creature dissappears either after their HPs hits 0, or their duration is spent.
* Remove the dead, or spent summoned creature from the list.
* Maybe keep track of the summoned creatures below the active battle list, just for long term interest.
* Clicking "Summon Creature" allows you to choose a caster, and add the creatures to the list, but it looks similar to Add Participant screen otherwise. You can't add a summoned creature to hold.
* Other then above, summoned creatures are just like any other MOB in the battle list.  They should have a "SUM" as their icon in place of PC for a character etc.
